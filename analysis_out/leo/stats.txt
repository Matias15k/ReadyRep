Notes
  * Rep speed values are EMG-timing proxies (burst timing), not bar velocity.
  * Sampling times are reconstructed as sample_count/1000 by tools/emg_temp.py; any error in the true rate scales every MDF value.
  * Absolute temperatures assume a 3.3 V supply and default ADC calibration; only changes are interpreted.
  * Many warm-up (light) reps fail the SNR gate, so H1's wu_k vs wu_1 comparison is reported but not relied on.
  * Primary MDF is computed over the whole rep. MDF moves by a median 29 Hz across one rep, so a single 500 ms window depends heavily on where it is placed; the lifting-window and high-activation results are listed above as sensitivity analyses.
  * Isolated spikes (> 8 x local robust SD) are zeroed before spectra; reps with >= 5 spike samples or any ADC-rail sample are excluded.
  * A pre-set temperature taken from the first seconds of the set (early_set) differs from an idle one by a median +0.02 °C; the idle-only sensitivity row shows its effect.
